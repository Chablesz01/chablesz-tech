ChableszShow v15 Bible text attribution

The bundled KJV.csv contains 31,102 verses in the traditional 66-book canon.
Text exported without wording changes from pythonbible-kjv 0.0.2 using
pythonbible 0.15.5. Square brackets marking supplied words in that source
are retained. No proprietary licensed translation is included.

Additional full Bible text datasets exported without wording changes using
pythonbible-asv, pythonbible-web, and pythonbible-ylt 0.0.2:
ASV: 66 books, 31,086 nonempty verses (16 omitted numbered slots).
WEB: 66 books, 31,102 verses, using its own chapter/verse metadata.
YLT: 66 books, 31,102 verses.
